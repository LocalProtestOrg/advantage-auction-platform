Show your items on your own website

Your website widget shows the items you have for sale on Advantage.Bid. It updates by itself when you add, change, sell or remove an item. Every item opens its own Advantage.Bid page, where buyers can see the details and buy securely. Your items also stay listed on Advantage.Bid.

How to add it to your website

  1. Click Copy Embed Code.
  2. In your website editor, add a block for custom HTML or embed code (WordPress: "Custom HTML" block; Squarespace: "Code" block; Wix: "Embed HTML" or "Embed a site"; Brilliant Directories: a custom HTML widget or content area).
  3. Paste the code, save, and publish your page.
  4. If your website does not allow embed code, use Copy Storefront Link and add it as a link or button instead.
