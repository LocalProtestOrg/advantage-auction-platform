It never registers with a state, files a return or pays tax: each of those is done by a person, and then recorded here. Reports are totals; no buyer details are shown.

Sales tax rules by state

Each state has one rule for every kind of sale: Advantage.Bid collects and remits (this needs a recorded active registration), or nothing is collected at checkout and the seller is responsible. A state without a rule is never permission to collect nothing.

Payments on hold and sales without tax

Auction payments held by the sales tax safeguard, and auctions paid without sales tax while the safeguard only reports. A hold ends when the state's rule and registration are completed, or when an exception is recorded below.

Exceptions

An exception lets one auction's payments proceed in one state without sales tax collected. It needs a written reason and is recorded with your name. Review each one with the accountant.
