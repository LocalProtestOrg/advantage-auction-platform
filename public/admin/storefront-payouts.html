Storefront Payouts

Weekly seller payouts for fixed-price storefront sales. Each record lists the paid, fulfilled, unrefunded and undisputed orders completed before the Wednesday 11:59 PM Eastern cutoff, with the seller's proceeds (item price plus shipping, less the flat 11% selling fee; sales tax is never seller proceeds). Records are prepared automatically on Thursday. Money moves only when you choose Pay Seller, by one transfer to the seller's connected payout account.
