Storefront Sales Tax Rules

Record, for each state, how sales tax on storefront purchases is handled, from professional tax advice: collected at checkout and remitted by Advantage.Bid (the tax is never paid to the seller); collected at checkout and paid to the seller with their proceeds (the seller remits); or not collected at checkout because the seller handles its own tax. Every storefront order is refused in a state without a rule here, even when no tax is calculated. When a rule says tax is collected at checkout, an order is also refused if tax is not actually being collected for that state (for example, no active registration with the payment processor). Every change is recorded.
