Sales Tax Rules

Record, for each state, how sales tax is handled, from professional tax advice. One rule covers every kind of sale in that state: auctions, marketplace purchases and purchases made on a seller's own website. There are two choices: collected at checkout and remitted by Advantage.Bid (the tax is never paid to the seller), or not collected at checkout because the seller is responsible for its own tax. A rule under which Advantage.Bid collects can be saved only after the active registration for that state is recorded on the Sales Tax page. A state without a rule is never permission to collect nothing: storefront orders there are refused, and auction payments are reported or held according to the safeguard setting. When a rule says tax is collected at checkout, a sale is also refused or held if tax is not actually being collected for that state. Every change is recorded.
