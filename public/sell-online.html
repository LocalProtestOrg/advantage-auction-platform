Questions

Professional Seller Storefront FAQ

Is there a monthly fee for the storefront?

No. There is no monthly storefront subscription and no listing fee. Advantage.Bid charges a flat 11% selling fee, which includes credit card processing, only when an item sells through Advantage.Bid checkout. The fee is calculated on the item price, not on shipping or sales tax. Your terms are set out in your Professional Seller agreement before you list.

Who can open a storefront?

Storefronts are for established businesses that sell professionally, such as shops, galleries, dealers and working artists and makers. Choose Retail Shop, Gallery or Studio when you sign up. Advantage.Bid reviews every business before its items can go live.

Do I need my own website?

No. Every storefront has its own page on Advantage.Bid, and your items appear in the Advantage.Bid marketplace. If you have a website, you can also show your storefront there with a copy-and-paste code.

Will the storefront work with my website builder?

The storefront code is a standard embed that works with most website builders that let you add embedded content or custom HTML. If your builder does not allow embeds, you can link to your Advantage.Bid storefront page instead.

How do I add my inventory?

Add items one at a time, or import a CSV or Excel spreadsheet of up to 2,000 items. You match your spreadsheet columns to the storefront fields, add photos by file name, link, bulk upload or ZIP file, review everything, and the items are created as drafts for you to finish.

Do my items have to appear on the Advantage.Bid marketplace?

Yes. Every published storefront item appears on your own website and in the Advantage.Bid marketplace. That is how your storefront reaches shoppers beyond your regular customers.

Do I have to ship?

You decide. You set a pickup location, and you choose whether your items offer shipping. If you offer shipping, you are responsible for packing and sending your items.

How do shoppers buy from my storefront?

Shoppers click Buy Now on an item, in your storefront on your website or on Advantage.Bid, and pay securely by card through Advantage.Bid checkout. You are notified of every order, you prepare the item for pickup or shipping, and your proceeds are paid to you by direct deposit.
