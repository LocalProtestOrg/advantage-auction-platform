Transparent Economics

Two products, kept separate and clear

Auction selling and fixed-price Storefront selling have different, clearly-stated economics. Your exact terms are documented in your Professional Seller agreement before you list.

Professional Auctions

You set your catalog, starting prices, and reserves. The buyer's premium is configurable and is yours, shown live to bidders during the auction.

Your professional platform terms are set on your account and disclosed in your Professional Seller agreement before you list — no surprise deductions, and sales tax is handled at checkout and never comes out of your proceeds.

Talk to Advantage.Bid about commercial terms for your business.

Professional Storefront (Buy Now)

Flat 11%

A flat 11% selling fee, including credit card processing, charged only when an item sells — on the item price (not shipping or tax). Nothing else is added on top.

No listing fees. You only pay when an item sells.

How payouts work

Advantage.Bid processes seller payouts weekly on Thursdays (bank settlement may take additional time afterward).

Auction sales: eligible proceeds are processed on the first applicable Thursday after pickup/fulfillment is complete and the proceeds are otherwise eligible.

Professional Storefront / Buy Now: eligible proceeds are processed weekly on Thursday for qualifying sales completed before the applicable Wednesday 11:59 PM cutoff.

Legitimate holds may apply for refunds, disputes, verification, or incomplete fulfillment/pickup.
