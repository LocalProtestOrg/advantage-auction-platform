1. Get your spreadsheet ready

Use your own spreadsheet or our template. One row per item; the first row names the columns. Items are imported as drafts: nothing is public until you publish it.
  • Required: Title and Price. Strongly recommended: a SKU (item number) on every row; photos are matched by SKU, and a repeat import skips items you already have.
  • Price in dollars, for example 125 or 1,250.00. Quantity is 1 per row (each storefront item sells once).
  • Ships: Yes uses your store shipping default; blank or No means pickup only. Pickup columns are optional; blank uses your storefront pickup location.
  • CSV or Excel (.xlsx), up to 2,000 items and 5 MB per file. Only the first worksheet is read, and formulas are imported as their saved values.
Download template (CSV) The template's example rows start with EXAMPLE-; delete them before importing.

2. Upload and check

Add photos by SKU

Name each photo after the item's SKU. Photos are added to your own draft, published or paused items only, and original files are kept at full quality.
  • A1001.jpg is the first photo of item A1001.
  • A1001_2.jpg, A1001-3.jpg or A1001 (4).jpg are more photos of the same item, in number order.
  • In a ZIP file, a folder named after the SKU works too: A1001/front.jpg, A1001/back.jpg.
  • JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP or HEIC, up to 10 MB each and 24 per item. A photo that matches no SKU is listed below and not uploaded. A photo already on the item is skipped.
