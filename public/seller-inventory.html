    Upload your spreadsheet

    Use the spreadsheet your auction or inventory software exports, as it is. You never need to rename columns: we match them in the next step. Items are imported as drafts.
    CSV or Excel (.xlsx), up to 2,000 items and 5 MB per file. Older Excel files (.xls): save them as .xlsx or CSV first. Download a template if you are starting from scratch.

    Importing drafts

    Add photos to items in your inventory

    Name each photo after the item's Inventory Number. Photos are added to your own draft, published or paused items only, and original files are kept at full quality.
    • A1001.jpg is the first photo of item A1001.
    • A1001_2.jpg, A1001-3.jpg or A1001 (4).jpg are more photos of the same item, in number order.
    • In a ZIP file, a folder named after the Inventory Number works too: A1001/front.jpg, A1001/back.jpg.
    • JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP or HEIC, up to 10 MB each and 24 per item. A photo that matches no item is listed below and not uploaded. A photo already on the item is skipped.
