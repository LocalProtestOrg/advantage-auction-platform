The details

What the free plan includes

  • No monthly membership fee — the business listing is free.
  • Promote up to 3 active upcoming events at a time (archive one to add another).
  • Up to 10 photos per event.
  • Featured/priority placement is not included in the free plan.
  • Running online auctions and selling directly (Buy Now) is Professional Seller, an optional upgrade — not part of the free listing.
