Advantage.Bid Auction Partner Program

For selected established estate sale companies and professional liquidators who want to add online auctions to their business.

  • 0% Advantage.Bid auction platform/software fee on qualifying auctions during the one-year program term.
  • You set and keep your buyer's premium.
  • Payment processing stays your responsibility and is passed through at actual cost, with no Advantage.Bid markup.
  • Your auctions are listed on the Advantage.Bid marketplace and can also appear on your own website where integrated.
  • Advantage.Bid processes eligible seller payouts every Thursday.
The program is by invitation. If Advantage.Bid has invited your company, open the personal link in your invitation to review the agreement and get started.
