# What is the buyer's premium?

The buyer's premium is a percentage added to your winning bid. You'll see the exact rate and your estimated total on each lot page before you bid.

It's 18% on auctions by individual sellers. Professional sellers set their own rate.

**Example (only an example):** a $100 winning bid with an 18% premium comes to $118, before any applicable sales tax.

Never state who receives the premium when talking to buyers. For a specific lot, always use that lot's live rate.
