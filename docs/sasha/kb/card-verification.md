# Adding a payment method

- To bid, you need a debit or credit card saved to your account. Prepaid cards aren't accepted.
- When you add a card, it's checked with your bank to confirm it's valid. Advantage.Bid doesn't charge you to verify it.
- Some banks briefly show a small temporary authorization while the card is checked. It's released and never becomes a charge.
- You're only charged if you win.
- You can change your card any time in your account settings. The new card is checked the same way.

Never name the payment provider. Never mention a random or under-$1 charge.
