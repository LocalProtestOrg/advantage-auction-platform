02Opportunity assessment

The directory already holds about 294 real, local companies with public contact details. After two years, none of them has logged in to the directory (Railway-side claims will be counted in the dry-run report). The biggest problem is the claim path, not awareness: the one button that should work leads nowhere. Most of the secure plumbing a claim channel needs is already built for the Event Partner programme. This channel therefore needs a front door, a sender and a separate lane. It does not need a new platform.

  • Why claimed listings matter to Advantage.Bid. A claimed company keeps its own page accurate, posts its sales, and brings buyers who follow that company. Each posted sale adds inventory to the marketplace at no cost. A claimed company is also the warmest possible audience for Professional Seller, because it already uses the platform.
  • What is realistic. Directory claim campaigns usually convert a small share of recipients. The value comes from the few who activate and post sales. Plan for a small, high-quality core, and don't count on mass adoption.
  • What would limit it. Trust (a cold email about "your listing" looks like a common scam pattern), data accuracy (G6, G7), free-mailbox deliverability, and small operators who mostly live on the phone.

Decision: run this as a slow, trust-first, low-volume channel. Measure it in claims and activated listings. Messages sent is not a success measure.

07Claim experience Build

Decision: the claim link opens a preview of the listing before any sign-in. A person sees their own company first and decides second.

  1. Email link goes to bid.advantage.bid/claim/<token>. Opening the page never uses up the token, so mail security scanners that pre-open links cannot break it.
  2. Preview page shows the listing as it appears today, which address the link was sent to (masked), "Claiming is free. No monthly fee. We never ask for payment details to claim.", and a phone number to call.
  3. One button: "This is my company, continue". Next screen: name and password only. The email address is fixed to the one invited.
  4. Claimed. The person lands on a five-step listing checklist.

Every preview page also offers four other answers: "This isn't my company", "This business has closed", "I'm not the right contact", "Please remove this listing". Each one stops all email to that address and is recorded. Removal requests are honored as a soft, reversible unpublish within two business days. Giving people an easy way out is a trust signal and protects deliverability.

08Verification experience

Decision: when someone redeems the emailed token, that counts as email verification. No separate "verify your email" step.

The token is single-use, bound to one address and delivered only to that inbox. That is the same proof a standard verification link gives. A new account is created with the email already verified, and the reason is recorded in the audit log. If an account already exists, the person signs in first. The existing proof ladder in verifyClaimProof does not change.

09Listing activation experience

After claiming, the person lands on a checklist that takes about ten minutes:

  1. Confirm business details: name, phone, website, and whether to show a street address or only a service area.
  2. Add a logo.
  3. Replace our description with one in your own words (300 characters or more).
  4. Choose service area and specialties.
  5. Post your next estate sale or auction, or mark "no sale scheduled right now".

14Reply and human-handoff logic

Decision: no automatic reply to a person, ever. This follows the Owner's standing rule that there is no autonomous response system for seller inquiries.

  • Replies go to l-<reply_key>@reply.advantage.bid, the inbound system already built for Event Partner, stored in the listing programme's own tables. The reply key matches the reply to the right company without anyone reading it first.
  • Handled without a person: unsubscribe wording (STOP, remove me, unsubscribe) suppresses the address; auto-replies are ignored; bounces are processed.
  • Everything else becomes a Toolbox task with the full thread, and a notification goes to info@advantage.bid. The Owner already monitors that inbox.
  • Service level: first human response within one business day. The task shows the SLA clock.
  • Legal, dispute or angry replies are escalated to the Owner and never answered from a template.

Company · location · website · phone · email (masked until opened) · listing status · journey · eligibility and reason · tier and score · outreach step, last and next send · last contact from any programme · next action and due date · engagement (visits, claim started) · claim status and proof · assigned rep · lock holder · Pro Seller status · notes.

Queues

Replies to answer · Claim help requests · Removal requests · Wrong-contact research · Tier A calls · Activation stalled · Pro Seller interest · Review decisions (ambiguous, data quality) · Disputes.

No double contact

A rep must take the company lock before logging or sending anything. Other reps see "Kym is working this company, since Tuesday". Locks lapse after 14 idle days. Taking the lock pauses the automated sequence. 1:1 rep email checks all suppression lists and the lock before sending.

Access

Marketing staff: view and work. Super Admin only: approve cohorts, change journeys, reassign locks. No package economics or financial fields appear anywhere in the tab.

Runs automatically

Identity linking on strong signals, screening, scoring, cohort proposals (the Director proposes, a person approves), sends inside an approved cohort, stop conditions, suppression, bounce and complaint processing, activation reminders, dashboard.

Fails closed

Ambiguous identity; suppression or eligibility lookup error; template or cohort not approved; caps reached; outside the send window; missing SES configuration set, From identity, postal address or unsubscribe secret; programme bounce rate over 3% or complaint rate over 0.1% (or any complaint before 50 deliveries), which pauses everything and alerts the Owner.

A person gets a task instead of another email

Any real reply, claim help, removal, dispute, wrong contact, Tier A call, stalled activation at day 21, Pro Seller interest.

Autonomy later: cohorts carry autonomous_allowed = false. After the pilot passes, the Owner can let the Director run approved-template cohorts within caps without approving each one.

20Attribution architecture

Attribution follows two paths so it survives lost cookies and devices.

  1. Hard link (primary): token, then message, then sequence, then cohort, then company. When a listing is claimed, organizations.acquisition records the journey, cohort, template version and proof method. When a member of that organization creates a Professional Seller profile, the record is copied to seller_profiles.acquisition. Seller conversion then carries the original journey with no guesswork.
  2. Session attribution (supporting): every link carries utm_source=advantage_bid&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=claimed_listing_<cohort>&utm_content=<template>_v<n>. The existing attribution service records the touch and stitches it to the account at sign-up.

New conversion keys are first-party only. Nothing is sent to advertising platforms.

23Abuse and failure protections

Listing takeover

  • Single-use tokens bound to one recipient, hashed
  • Opening the page never consumes the token
  • Rate-limited self-requests; the address is masked
  • Help requests confirmed by calling the listing's own phone
  • Name, domain and contact changes reviewed; old address notified
  • Every attempt logged; repeated denials alert staff

Looking like phishing

  • No attachments, no forms inside email
  • Links only to advantage.bid domains
  • "We never ask for payment details to claim" on page and in replies
  • Real phone number and a named person
  • Confirm DMARC enforcement on advantage.bid (infra check)

Sending failures

  • Idempotency key per company, cycle and step, so there are no double sends
  • SES errors retry with backoff and never skip a step
  • Health thresholds auto-pause
  • Config missing means nothing sends

Bad data

  • Data Quality review rule
  • One send per address when several listings share it
  • Wrong-contact and closed-business exits
  • Removal honored within two business days

24Recommended pilot design

  • Size: 50 companies. Houston metro and NYC Tri-State first (about 60 listings before exclusions). If fewer than 50 are eligible, fill with national Tier A estate sale companies.
  • Pace: 10 E1 sends per day over five send days. The full sequence finishes in about three weeks.
  • Pre-work for every pilot company: factual description replaces the generated text; badge problems fixed; data quality reviewed by a person.
  • One version of each email. 50 recipients is too few for an A/B test to mean anything. Pilot learning comes from what replies say, not from comparing variants.
  • No holdout group. The baseline is close to zero: no directory logins in two years. The dry-run confirms the Railway claim count.
  • Rep: Kym holds all 50, with Tier A call tasks.
  • Readout: 30 days after the last E1, and again at 60 days for activation.
