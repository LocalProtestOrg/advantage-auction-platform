# Advantage.Bid Official Logo Assets

## Purpose

This folder contains the authoritative Advantage.Bid logo assets for
marketing, advertising, social media, platform creative, sales
materials, and other approved brand uses.

These files are source-of-truth brand assets. They are not creative
references to be redrawn.

## Core Rule

**Always use an approved logo file from this folder when displaying the
Advantage.Bid logo.**

Do not: - regenerate the logo with an image model - redraw or
approximate the logo - typeset `Advantage.Bid` as a substitute for the
logo when a logo treatment is intended - alter the gavel, AB mark,
wordmark, proportions, or tagline - invent a new logo variation -
stretch, distort, crop, or obscure the logo - change brand colors merely
to match a creative

The creative system may choose the appropriate approved logo variant for
the placement and background, then composite the actual asset into the
finished creative.

## Approved Variants

### Primary Horizontal Logo

Use the primary horizontal logo for: - advertisements - marketing
graphics - headers - presentations - seller and buyer acquisition
creative - larger brand placements - light or neutral backgrounds where
the standard logo remains clear

This is the preferred full-brand treatment when sufficient horizontal
space is available.

### Primary Transparent Logo

Use the transparent version when: - the logo must be composited over an
approved background - a white rectangular background would interfere
with the design - the underlying background provides sufficient contrast

Preserve clear space around the logo and ensure every element remains
legible.

### White Horizontal Logo

Use the white logo primarily for: - dark navy backgrounds - dark
photographic areas - dark marketing panels - other backgrounds where the
primary dark lettering would lose contrast

Do not use the white version on a light background where it becomes
difficult to read.

### AB Icon

Use the AB icon for: - social profile imagery - avatars - app/icon
treatments - compact placements - square or near-square placements -
locations where the full horizontal logo would become too small to read

Do not substitute the AB icon for the full logo when the placement has
sufficient room and the full Advantage.Bid identity is important.

## Brand Prominence

Logo presence alone is not sufficient.

The selected logo must remain clearly recognizable at the size at which
the finished creative will actually be viewed, including normal mobile
and social-feed viewing sizes.

For Advantage.Bid-led creative, the Advantage.Bid identity should be
immediately recognizable.

For Professional Seller-led event creative, the Professional Seller
remains the primary identity where appropriate, but the Advantage.Bid
association must still be intentional, legible, and clearly visible.

Do not make Advantage.Bid branding microscopic merely to satisfy a
technical logo-presence requirement.

## Taglines

Use the campaign-appropriate approved messaging.

Seller-facing campaign slogan:

**The Smarter Way to Sell.**

Buyer-facing campaign slogan:

**The Smarter Way to Shop.**

The full primary brand logo may contain its established brand tagline,
**GET THE ADVANTAGE**. Do not alter the artwork embedded in an approved
logo asset.

Campaign slogans are separate creative messaging and should not be baked
into or substituted for the official logo artwork unless an approved
logo variant specifically contains them.

## Capitalization

Use the official brand spelling:

**Advantage.Bid**

For major marketing headlines and labels, use natural title-style
capitalization where appropriate.

Examples: - Have Items to Sell? - Turn Your Items Into Cash. - You Can
Do This. - It's Built to Be Easy. - Help Is Here. - Estate Sale - Online
Auction

Do not default important creative headlines to all lowercase or all
uppercase unless a specific approved design calls for it.

## Creative Integration

The recommended production sequence is:

1.  Select or generate the non-brand creative composition.
2.  Complete merchandise, environmental, typography, and layout work.
3.  Select the correct approved logo variant.
4.  Composite the actual logo asset into the final creative.
5.  Run brand-prominence and legibility QA.
6.  Verify the logo has not been altered or approximated.

Image generation should not be relied upon to reproduce the
Advantage.Bid logo accurately.

## Asset Integrity

Treat the files in this folder as protected brand assets.

Creative references may teach composition, hierarchy, spacing,
merchandise treatment, and visual style. They must not supersede these
official logo assets.

If a generated advertisement contains an AI-created or typeset
approximation of the Advantage.Bid logo, replace it with an approved
asset before the creative can pass final QA.

## Future Assets

Additional official logo variants may be added to this folder later.

New files should not automatically become authoritative merely because
they appear in the directory. They should be explicitly identified as
approved Advantage.Bid brand assets before production use.

When there is uncertainty about which logo variant is appropriate,
prefer the approved primary logo that provides the strongest legibility
and brand recognition without overwhelming the campaign hierarchy.
