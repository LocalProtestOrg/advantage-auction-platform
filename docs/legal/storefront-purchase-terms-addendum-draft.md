## Fixed-Price Purchase Terms

These terms apply when you buy an item at a fixed price ("Buy Now") from a Professional Seller's storefront, whether you
find the item on Advantage.Bid or on the seller's own website. They are part of the Advantage.Bid Buyer Terms.

### 1. How these terms fit together

When you buy a fixed-price item, three sets of terms apply:

1. **These Fixed-Price Purchase Terms.**
2. **The seller's own policies**: the seller's Store Terms and the seller's Shipping & Pickup Policy, shown to you before
   you pay.
3. **The Advantage.Bid Terms of Service and Buyer Terms**, for everything not covered here (your account, acceptable use,
   privacy and the general provisions).

If they conflict, these Fixed-Price Purchase Terms control, then the seller's policies, then the general terms. Provisions
of the general terms that are written for auctions (bidding, buyer's premium, auction pickup windows, abandoned auction
lots, auction refund requests, and the fees in Schedule A) do not apply to fixed-price purchases. **[Attorney review]**

A seller's policies cannot take away the protections in sections 7, 10, 12 and 14 of these terms.

### 2. Who you are buying from

You are buying from the Professional Seller named on the item page. Advantage.Bid operates the marketplace and checkout,
processes your payment and keeps the record of your order. The seller is responsible for the item, its description, its
condition, and for handing it over at pickup or shipping it.

### 3. Your order and payment

- The total shown on the review screen before you pay is the item price, any shipping charge and any sales tax. There is
  no buyer's premium on fixed-price purchases.
- You pay by debit or credit card. Prepaid cards are not accepted.
- While you check out, the item is reserved for you for up to 30 minutes.
- Your purchase is complete when your payment succeeds and you see the order confirmation with an order number. If the
  seller changed the price, shipping charge or its policies while you were checking out, you are asked to review the order
  again before anything is charged.
- In the rare case that a seller cannot supply an item after you paid (for example it was sold elsewhere), your payment is
  refunded in full.

### 4. Sales are final after payment

Once your payment succeeds, you cannot cancel the purchase simply because you changed your mind.

This does not limit your rights to a refund or other remedy when:

- the item is materially different from its listing (section 7);
- the item is not delivered or not made available for pickup as promised;
- the seller cannot or does not complete the sale;
- the seller's own Store Terms allow a return or cancellation; or
- applicable law gives you a right to cancel, a refund or another remedy.

### 5. The seller's policies

Every seller must publish Store Terms and a Shipping & Pickup Policy before you can buy from them. Both are linked on the
item page and on the review screen, and you confirm you have read them before you pay. The exact versions shown to you are
saved with your order and do not change if the seller later updates them.

### 6. Returns

Ordinary returns (for example, the item fits differently than you hoped, or you no longer want it) follow the seller's
Store Terms. If the seller does not accept returns, ordinary returns are not available. Section 7 still applies.

### 7. Items materially different from their listing

Whatever the seller's own return policy says, including "all sales final" or "sold as is", you may report an item that is
**materially different from its listing**, and Advantage.Bid will review it.

An item is materially different from its listing when a significant fact in the listing is untrue or a significant
problem was left out, for example:

- it is a different item, maker, material, era, size or quantity than described;
- it is a reproduction, copy or replacement presented as an original;
- it has damage, a defect, a missing part or a repair that the listing did not disclose and that significantly affects
  its value or use; or
- it does not work when the listing said it does.

An item is **not** materially different because of wear, condition or flaws that the listing described or that are
clearly visible in its photos, normal signs of age or use for that kind of item, small differences in color caused by
screens or lighting, or because it does not meet expectations the listing did not create.

When you report such a problem, Advantage.Bid holds the seller's payment for your order while it reviews the report. If
Advantage.Bid finds the item materially different from its listing, it may issue a full or partial refund or arrange
another appropriate remedy. It may ask you to return the item, in the same condition, as described in section 11.

### 8. Pickup and shipping

**Pickup.** Collect your item at the location, in the time and in the way stated in the seller's Shipping & Pickup Policy.
Bring the order confirmation and identification if the seller requires it. The seller confirms the pickup in its order
records.

**Shipping.** When a seller offers shipping, the seller ships to the address you gave at checkout and records the carrier
and tracking number. The shipping charge is shown before you pay. The seller records delivery once the carrier shows the
item delivered.

### 9. Items that are not collected

What happens when an item is not collected follows the seller's Shipping & Pickup Policy, which you see before you pay.
That includes pickup deadlines and any storage charge, forfeiture or disposal the seller's policy states. Advantage.Bid
does not apply a deadline or forfeiture of its own. **[Attorney review]**

A seller may enforce only terms that its policy disclosed before you paid and that applicable law allows. If you cannot
collect an item, contact the seller or Advantage.Bid as soon as you can.

### 10. Reporting a problem

Report any problem from **My Purchases** (Report a problem). Please report problems you can discover by looking at or
using the item:

- within **48 hours after you pick it up**, for a pickup purchase; or
- within **7 calendar days after it is delivered**, for a shipped purchase.

These periods start when the pickup or delivery is recorded for your order, not when you paid. Your order page shows
the date.

These are reporting expectations that help everyone resolve problems quickly. They are not a deadline that cancels your
rights. A later report is still reviewed on its merits, and these periods do not limit your rights under applicable law,
including for fraud, hidden defects you could not reasonably have discovered in that time, items that never arrived, or
your rights with your card issuer. If the pickup or delivery time is unknown or uncertain, Advantage.Bid reviews the report
from the facts available. Report an item that never arrived, or a pickup that could not happen, at any time.
**[Attorney review]**

### 11. How Advantage.Bid reviews a problem

Advantage.Bid reviews the report, hears from you and the seller, and may ask for photos or other information. While the
review is open, the seller is not paid for your order. Advantage.Bid then decides the outcome, which may be a full refund,
a partial refund, a return and refund, or no refund. If a return is needed, Advantage.Bid tells you how; the seller is
responsible for the cost of returning an item that was materially different from its listing. **[Attorney review]**

Opening a report is not a finding against you or the seller.

### 12. Refunds

- Refunds go back to the card you paid with.
- A full refund includes the item price, any shipping charge and any sales tax you paid.
- **No refund fee.** Advantage.Bid does not charge a cancellation, restocking, administrative or processing fee because a
  refund is issued, and does not deduct its card processing costs from your refund.
- A seller's Store Terms may describe its own return conditions for ordinary returns (section 6); they cannot add a fee to
  a refund for an item that was materially different from its listing, not delivered, or not supplied.

Your bank may take several business days to show the refund.

### 13. Card disputes (chargebacks)

You keep any right you have to dispute a charge with your card issuer. We ask that you report a problem to us first
(section 10), because most problems are resolved faster that way. While a card dispute is open, the seller is not paid
for that order.

### 14. Your rights under the law

Nothing in these terms limits any right you have under applicable law that cannot be waived or limited by agreement,
including consumer protection law.

### 15. Sales tax

Sales tax, if any, is calculated at checkout from the pickup location or the shipping address and shown before you pay.

### 16. Changes

Advantage.Bid may update these terms. The version in effect when you place an order applies to that order.

## Review notes (not part of the terms)

### What the system does today (branch `feat/storefront-purchase-policies`)

| Term | How it is enforced |
|---|---|
| 1, 5: both seller policies required, versions saved | The server refuses an order (and the quote) unless the seller's Store Terms and Shipping & Pickup Policy are both published and shown; the exact versions are stored on the order and can never change (database rule, migration 203). The item page shows Buy Now only when both are published. |
| 3: total, cards, 30-minute hold, price-change re-review | Existing checkout (unchanged). |
| 4: final after payment | There is no buyer cancellation route; refunds are made only by an administrator, with a written reason kept in the order history. |
| 7, 11: misdescription review and hold | A buyer report of "not as described", "damaged", "not received" or "refund request" holds the seller's payout automatically; only an administrator releases it, and not while another report or a card dispute on the order is open. |
| 9: no platform deadline | No process completes an order or makes it payable because time passes. An order becomes payable only after the seller records the pickup or delivery. |
| 10: 48 hours / 7 days | Measured from the pickup or delivery time recorded in the order's permanent history (it cannot be altered). Shown to buyer and seller. Never used to refuse or close a report; the reviewer sees whether a report came within, after or before the period. |
| 10, 11: seller not paid during review | The weekly payout includes an order only after its reporting period has ended, and never while it is on hold or under review. |
| 12: no refund fee | An administrator refund returns the full amount paid (item, shipping, tax). No fee is deducted. The seller-fault card-cost recovery switch stays off. |
| 13: disputes | A card dispute holds the payout; the hold cannot be released while the dispute is open. |

### Relationship to the other documents

- **Buyer Terms v3 (published).** Auction-only ("By registering to bid"). It says nothing about fixed-price purchases,
  yet checkout links it. Its section 8 ("All items are sold AS-IS, WHERE-IS") would, if applied to storefront sales,
  conflict with the misdescription protection in section 7 here. Section 1 here makes the fixed-price terms control.
- **Terms of Service (published /terms.html, Last Updated October 7, 2026).** Written for auctions. Section 22 (items not
  picked up "deemed abandoned and forfeited without refund"; refunds only for material misrepresentation "in its sole
  discretion"; refund requests "before leaving the pickup location"; "processing fees may apply") and **Schedule A**
  (Processing Fee of $20 or 30% on an approved refund, Chargeback Fee, Storage and Disposal Fees) conflict with policies 2,
  4 and 5 if applied to fixed-price purchases. Section 1 here excludes them for fixed-price purchases.
- **Terms of Service / Buyer Terms v4 draft (unpublished, docs/legal/terms-of-service-v4.md).** Carries the same
  section 22 and Schedule A, and section 19 (no chargeback without first contacting Advantage.Bid; chargeback fees). Not
  changed here. When v4 is finalized, either fold these fixed-price terms into it or add an express carve-out for
  fixed-price purchases; v4 must not be published without one.
- **Seller Agreement v4 (published 2026-11-06).** Supports these terms: 3.2 and 3.3 (the seller is responsible for
  accurate descriptions and disclosing known defects), 7.3 (storefront payouts follow "the fulfillment and eligibility
  rules the platform actually enforces"; payouts may be held for disputes and review), 7.5 (withholding and offset for
  refunds and disputes). Gaps: it does not expressly say that a Professional Seller's Store Terms cannot override the
  Advantage.Bid misdescription protection, or that Advantage.Bid decides storefront problem reports. 8.4 (Advantage
  handles uncollected property) is written for auctions and differs from policy 2 (the seller's policy governs).
- **Seller Store Terms and Shipping & Pickup Policies.** Sellers write their own; Advantage.Bid does not write or legally
  review them, and can withdraw one with a written reason. The seller policy editor now tells sellers what each must cover
  (returns; pickup arrangements, deadline and what happens to uncollected items).

### Flagged for attorney review

1. **Order of precedence and the auction carve-out (section 1).** Whether the published Terms of Service and Buyer Terms v3
   need an amendment, or whether these terms alone can exclude their auction provisions for fixed-price purchases.
2. **Seller-set forfeiture, storage and disposal (section 9).** Enforceability of a seller's forfeiture of a paid item
   without refund under Texas and other states' law, required notice, and whether Advantage.Bid should set limits (for
   example a minimum notice before forfeiture). Lewis & Maese's published policy says "Any Item(s) left after 30 days will
   be forfeited" with no notice or refund terms.
3. **Reporting periods (section 10).** Wording that sets expectations without operating as a waiver or limitation period
   (Texas DTPA and other state consumer laws; implied warranties for merchant sellers; the effect of "as is" disclaimers
   on consumer sales).
4. **Misdescription remedy and returns (sections 7 and 11).** The definition of "materially different", who pays return
   shipping, and whether Advantage.Bid's decision should be described as final between buyer and seller.
5. **Chargebacks (section 13).** Whether any improper-chargeback cost recovery should apply to fixed-price purchases (it is
   excluded here with the rest of Schedule A).
6. **Marketplace facilitator role.** Whether "you are buying from the seller" (section 2) fits Advantage.Bid's role as the
   merchant on the card statement and the sales tax decisions.
