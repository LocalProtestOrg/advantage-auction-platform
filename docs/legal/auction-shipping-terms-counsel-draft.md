# Auction shipping terms: framework for counsel (DRAFT, 2026-11-04; not legal advice; not published)

Purpose: when Limited Shipping is enabled for auctions (roadmap item C), the Seller Agreement and the Buyer Terms need
shipping terms that place fulfillment, packaging, loss, damage and claims on the seller **to the maximum extent the law
allows**. This document sets out the intended allocation and the questions counsel must answer. Wording marked
`[COUNSEL TO DRAFT]` is deliberately left for counsel. It does not assume Advantage.Bid can remove obligations the law
places on it.

## 1. Intended allocation
| Topic | Responsibility (intended) | Notes for counsel |
|---|---|---|
| Whether to offer shipping | Seller, within platform rules (verified Professional, or admin-approved Individual) | Platform may refuse or withdraw shipping at any time |
| Packing and packaging | Seller | Adequate for the item and carrier; fragile and high-value standards `[COUNSEL TO DRAFT]` |
| Carrier choice, tracking, insurance | Seller | Tracking required to release payout (platform rule); insurance for items above a value `[DECISION]` |
| Shipping charges shown to buyers | Seller sets them; the platform displays them before bidding | Charges must match the pre-bid estimate |
| Handling time | Seller (stated per auction or template) | Late shipment consequences `[COUNSEL TO DRAFT]` |
| Loss or damage in transit | Seller, until delivery to the buyer | Risk-of-loss transfer point under the UCC for shipment vs destination contracts `[COUNSEL]` |
| Buyer claims (not received, damaged, not as described) | Seller resolves; refunds from seller proceeds | Platform's role: claims process, payout holds, recovery from future payouts |
| Chargebacks on shipped orders | Seller bears the loss where the seller is at fault | Allocation vs card-network rules `[COUNSEL]` |
| Prohibited and restricted items | Seller complies (hazmat, firearms, ivory, alcohol, etc.) | Item list and penalties `[COUNSEL TO DRAFT]` |
| Customs and export | Seller (if international is ever allowed; default domestic only) | |
| Sales tax on shipping | As the tax rules require (CPA) | Never assumed |
| Indemnity | Seller indemnifies Advantage.Bid for shipping-related claims caused by the seller | Scope and caps `[COUNSEL]` |

## 2. What the platform cannot simply disclaim (counsel to confirm)
- Consumer-protection laws (unfair or deceptive practices, mail-order rules such as the FTC Mail, Internet, or
  Telephone Order Merchandise Rule where it applies to the party that is the seller of record).
- Card-network chargeback rules: the merchant of record bears chargebacks as against the card network, whatever the
  contract with the seller says; the contract can only shift the cost to the seller afterwards.
- Statutory warranty or marketplace-facilitator obligations that attach to Advantage.Bid by its role.
- Duties arising from Advantage.Bid's own conduct (for example, its own errors in displaying shipping terms).

## 3. Platform controls that support the allocation (technical; roadmap C)
Shipping off by default; verified-seller gate; admin approval for Individual Sellers; tracking required before payout;
payout hold window for shipped lots; recovery of refunds from future payouts (existing mechanism); claims record per
order; seller education before first use.

## 4. Questions for counsel
1. Is Advantage.Bid the seller of record, the seller's agent, or a marketplace for auction lots that ship? The answer
   decides which consumer rules attach to whom.
2. Shipment contract or destination contract by default, and how to state the risk-of-loss transfer clearly to buyers.
3. Enforceability of the seller indemnity and of recovering refunds and chargebacks from future payouts.
4. Required buyer-facing disclosures before bidding (shipping charges, handling time, who is responsible for claims).
5. Whether Individual Sellers who ship need different terms from Professional Sellers.
6. Insurance: require it above a value, or disclose that items are uninsured?
