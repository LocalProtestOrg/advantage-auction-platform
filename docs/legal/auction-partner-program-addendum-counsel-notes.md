without inventing new legal policy, and what still needs a decision. Not legal advice.

| Item | How the addendum handles it | Still needed |

Pre-existing for all sellers; not specific to the Program. |

For Qualifying Auctions the addendum controls on conflict. | Owner/counsel: update §1.3 and §6.8 of the Seller Agreement in a future version so it matches the professional self-publish model for all professional sellers. Not changed here (out of scope). |
| Seller Agreement liability cap (§11.2) | Unchanged. Note: the cap is "the total commission actually earned by Advantage" on the auction; with a 0% platform fee that amount may be zero for Qualifying Auctions. | Counsel to review (already marked for counsel review in the Seller Agreement). |
