# SEO infrastructure audit — bid.advantage.bid and www.advantage.bid (2026-11-11)

Read-only audit of both websites, followed by the four owner-approved platform fixes (2, 3, 5, 6). This record is
corrected for the Brilliant Directories sitemap (see section 2).

The two properties:
- **bid.advantage.bid**: the auction and marketplace platform (this repository). The canonical source for auctions,
  lots, items and storefronts.
- **www.advantage.bid**: the Brilliant Directories (BD) marketing and directory site, managed by the owner in BD.

## 1. What already worked on bid.advantage.bid

- Home: title, description, self-canonical, Open Graph/Twitter, WebSite + SearchAction + Organization JSON-LD.
- `/shop` and category pages: server-rendered items with prices and canonical links; CollectionPage + ItemList +
  BreadcrumbList.
- Item pages `/shop/item/:id/:slug`: Product + Offer, absolute images, breadcrumb; legacy and mistyped URLs 301 to the
  canonical URL; unknown items 404; sold items SoldOut.
- Storefronts `/pro/:slug`: LocalBusiness/Organization JSON-LD and canonical. Event pages: server-rendered body and
  Event/Place JSON-LD (street address withheld).
- http to https 301; apex to www 301 (BD); `/how-it-works.html` 301 to `/how-it-works`. Private pages redirect to sign
  in; sign-in and widget frames are noindex. robots.txt declares the sitemap. No visible "AI" or vendor wording in
  metadata.

## 2. www.advantage.bid (Brilliant Directories)

**Sitemap (corrected).** The audit first reported the BD sitemap missing because it checked the conventional
`/sitemap.xml`. BD publishes its sitemap at
`https://www.advantage.bid/filedata/cache/xml-sitemaps/sitemap.xml`, it has already been submitted to Google Search
Console, and BD confirms it does not need a footer link. Nothing about its location or submission should change, and
no duplicate sitemap is needed.

Verification 2026-11-11 (read-only): HTTP 200, `application/xml`, a valid `sitemapindex` of 18 child sitemaps (all
HTTP 200), 2,208 URLs, all on `https://www.advantage.bid`. A sample of 12 URLs across page types returned 200 with
`index, follow`; 10 of 12 were self-canonical.

**Content observations (separate from the location; for the owner to consider in BD, no action taken):**
- About 600 listed URLs are member form pages (`/connect` 295, `/writeareview` 303) marked `index, follow`. They are
  thin and near-identical; BD may offer a setting to leave them out of the sitemap or mark them noindex.
- 33 URLs appear more than once across the child sitemaps (duplicates are harmless but untidy).
- Some state/city URLs are listed in a form that canonicalizes elsewhere (for example `/hawaii/honolulu` declares
  `/honolulu` canonical). Listing only canonical URLs is the recommended practice.

**robots.txt (owner handles separately in BD).** It allows Googlebot, Slurp (Yahoo), msnbot, Bing Preview and
Twitterbot, then ends with `User-agent: *` / `Disallow: /`, which blocks every other crawler (for example DuckDuckBot
and Applebot). Google crawling is not affected. There is no `Sitemap:` line; that is optional because the sitemap is
submitted in Search Console.

**Other BD notes.** `/all-events` shows listings only inside embedded frames (no indexable listing text on BD); the
Houston city page title uses the older brand name and shows an unrelated advertisement.

## 4. The approved fixes (2026-11-11)

- **2. Sitemap and metadata visibility.** One added predicate in the canonical visibility module,
  `indexableNativeAuctionSql` (the shared marketplace rule plus completed `closed` sales). The sitemap and every
  auction/lot page use it: demo, hidden (non-syndicated), archived and unpublished auctions are never listed, and a
  page viewable by link but not indexable gets `noindex,follow`. Withdrawn lots are excluded and noindex. Legitimate
  completed auctions and their lots stay listed and indexable (owner historical content policy). The Marketplace
  Integrity Suite now checks that the sitemap's auction and lot counts equal the shared rule's counts.
- **3. Not-found responses.** Unknown or non-public auctions, lots and events, and unknown or unpublished storefronts,
  answer HTTP 404 with `noindex` and no canonical; the page itself still loads and shows its "not found" message. A
  database error never produces a 404 (the page fails open as before). Valid URLs and redirects are unchanged.
- **5. Structured data and social metadata.** Open lots: Product + Offer valid until the lot closes. Closed and withdrawn
  lots: no Product node (title, description, image and breadcrumb remain). Event breadcrumb points to `/events.html`.
  Missing og:type, og:site_name, twitter:card and image alt text are added without duplicating tags a page already has.
  Item pages and their sharing are untouched.
- **6. Home page and internal linking.** The menu links to Shop items for sale and Estate sales & events, and How it
  works uses its canonical URL. The home page's existing no-JavaScript block now carries server-rendered links to live
  and upcoming auctions, estate sales and events, and newest items, chosen only by the shared rules (the integrity
  suite checks the home auction links against them). The visible design is unchanged.
